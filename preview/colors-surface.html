Rules
✓Admin: Borders are required alongside tonal layering — they act as structural scan-path guides, like grid lines on a dive computer.
✓Diver-facing: No 1px line rule. Define sections via background shifts only. Border exception: ghost border rgba(232,228,219,0.15) when two same-bg containers must separate.
✗Never use pure black (#000000) anywhere. Use ocean-900 (#0a2540) for maximum emphasis.
✗Never mix depth strategies — borders AND shadows on the same element break the visual grammar.
