Section 3 — Gas Management
Understanding Nitrox
Nitrox diving, or EANx diving, involves using a breathing gas mixture with a higher proportion of oxygen than standard air. This reduces nitrogen loading, extending your no-decompression limits significantly at recreational depths.
Before diving nitrox, you must verify the oxygen percentage in your tank and set your dive computer accordingly. Your maximum operating depth (MOD) is calculated from the partial pressure of oxygen — never exceed 1.4 bar PO₂ during recreational dives.
