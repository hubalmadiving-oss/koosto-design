Validation squares

The unique square buttons that appear on every diver row in Planning · Today · Validation mode. Single-select: the operator picks one square per diver to record the outcome — red × (cancelled / no-show), a numbered middle square for partial completion (N out of total accomplished), or green ✓ (all planned dives accomplished). Order of dives doesn't matter — only the final count. Strip persists as buttons until the operator validates the whole trip card; then every row collapses to a resolved pill.

Visual types The visual slot takes one of four shapes. Pick by what the number means, not by decoration.
Shape Use for How it's computed
line A trend over time — the direction matters. Utilization, bookings, fleet value. Last N period buckets of the metric, min→max normalized. Spark = no axes; promote to full line chart (with axes + legend) when the shape itself is the insight.
bar Discrete comparison across categories — per-type counts, per-day volume. When buckets are few and independent. One bar per category, shared y-scale. Stacked bars when sub-states sum to the whole (avail/lent/issue).
bar (linear) A ratio / fill 0–100% — utilization, capacity, completion. One value against its max. value ÷ max × 100. Segment it when sub-parts matter; always label both ends.
ring A single headline ratio you want to feel "complete/incomplete" — readiness, % serviced. One number, emphatic. Same value ÷ max as linear, drawn as arc. Use sparingly — one ring per card max, never a grid of them.
